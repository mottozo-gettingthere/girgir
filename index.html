How to connect

  1. 1 · Same Wi-Fi

    Put both phones on one Wi-Fi, or on one phone’s hotspot. No internet needed.

  2. 2 · Create

    One phone taps Create channel and shows its code.

  3. 3 · Scan both ways

    The other taps Join channel and scans it, then shows its own code back. The first phone reads it by itself.

  4. 4 · Talk

    Linked. Earphones on each phone work best.

Use it offline

Android: browser menu → Install or Add to Home screen.

iPhone, iPad: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.

Open it once from the home screen while online. After that it needs no internet.

Talk modes

Open — microphone always live. Best with earphones.

Latch — tap to talk, tap again to stop.

Hold — live only while pressed. Best on a speaker.

Each phone picks its own.

More than two phones

+ Person on a linked phone shows a new code. The new phone taps Join channel and scans it, then this phone scans their reply.

That phone becomes the hub: it passes everyone's voice, Morse and pings to everyone else, so it must stay in the channel. Up to five extra people per hub.

Tap × on a name to take that phone off the channel.

What the readouts mean
Bars, top right
Link quality, worked out from round-trip time, packet loss and jitter. Four bars is a clean link.
LINK
Round-trip time and the share of audio packets being lost.
BATT / THEIRS
Battery on each phone. The iPhone cannot report its own level to a web app, so it shows a dash there.
INPUT
Which microphone is actually open — EARBUD, WIRED or BUILT-IN — so a headset that failed to connect is obvious rather than silent.
NET
The network you share, and each phone's last number. No browser can read a Wi-Fi name, so this is how you confirm you are on the same one. Amber means different networks, and audio will not flow.
OUT
Where their voice comes out. A picker appears where the phone allows one; otherwise it names what the system does — earpiece on iPhone, loudspeaker on Android.
Meters
Live audio level — yours in amber, theirs in teal. Set a name in settings and the labels use it instead of YOU and THEM.

Storage on this phone

Only the app is kept, so it runs offline. No logs, no history, no recordings. It remembers just your name and that it is installed.

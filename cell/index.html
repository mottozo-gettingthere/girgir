How to connect

  1. 1 · Mobile data on

    Both phones online: 4G, 5G or any Wi-Fi with internet. You can be anywhere.

  2. 2 · Send an invite

    One phone taps Create channel, then Send invite, and picks WhatsApp, AirDrop, Bluetooth or anything else in the share sheet.

  3. 3 · Reply

    The other opens the link and taps Join, then Send reply back the same way.

  4. 4 · Talk

    The first phone opens the reply link, or taps Paste reply. Linked. Send it within about two minutes.

  5. Signal gone?

    If the link drops and you are near each other, Girgir 5G sends you to Girgir, the Wi-Fi version, to carry on with no internet. Keep both installed.

Install it

Android: browser menu → Install or Add to Home screen.

iPhone, iPad: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.

Install Girgir (Wi-Fi) the same way. They are two separate apps that point to each other.

Talk modes

Open — microphone always live. Best with earphones.

Latch — tap to talk, tap again to stop.

Hold — live only while pressed. Best on a speaker.

Each phone picks its own.

More than two phones

+ Person on a linked phone makes a new invite. Send it to the new phone; it opens the link, taps Join and sends its reply back.

That phone becomes the hub: it passes everyone's voice, Morse and pings to everyone else, so it must stay in the channel. Up to five extra people per hub.

Everyone in the channel is a circle with their initial. A ring in their colour grows while they talk.

On the hub, tap × on a circle to take that phone off the channel.

What the readouts mean
Bars, top right
Link quality, worked out from round-trip time, packet loss and jitter. Four bars is a clean link.
LINK
Round-trip time and the share of audio packets being lost.
BATT / THEIRS
Battery on each phone. The iPhone cannot report its own level to a web app, so it shows a dash there.
INPUT
Which microphone is actually open — EARBUD, WIRED or BUILT-IN — so a headset that failed to connect is obvious rather than silent.
NET
How the voice travels. direct: phone to phone over the internet. same Wi-Fi: both on one network. relay: through the relay server in settings, still encrypted.
OUT
Where their voice comes out. A picker appears where the phone allows one; otherwise it names what the system does — earpiece on iPhone, loudspeaker on Android.
Meters
Live audio level — yours in amber, theirs in teal. Set a name in settings and the labels use it instead of YOU and THEM.

Storage on this phone

No logs, no history, no recordings. It remembers your name (shared with Girgir), that it is installed, and a relay if you add one. Invites travel only through the apps you send them with; no Girgir server sees them.
